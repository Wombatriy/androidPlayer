BPM Player — a focused music player for dancers, DJs and everyone who likes to control the tempo of their favorite tracks.

Change tempo without changing pitch
Slow music down or speed it up while preserving its musical key. Rubber Band R3 audio processing makes tempo changes smoother, particularly when adjusting a track slightly for dance practice or a set.

Know the BPM
Automatically estimate the tempo of local tracks, compare it with BPM values embedded in filenames, tap along to measure it, or enter a correction manually.

Practice and prepare
Set A–B loop points, navigate the waveform, jump forward and backward by 10 seconds, and build playlists in any order. Shuffle a selected playlist when you want a fresh sequence.

Keep listening
Music keeps playing in the background, with transport controls available through Android's media notification and lock screen.

Your own music library
Choose a folder using Android's file picker. BPM Player reads supported audio files from your device without uploading your music to a service.

Designed for dancers, dance instructors, DJs and active listening.

Notes: Automatic BPM estimation is approximate and may need manual correction for complex rhythms. Playback quality depends on the recording and device.
